Hi, my name is Shahar Glazner! I'm a software engineer who loves building
products, teams and open source software.

I founded [Keep](https://github.com/keephq/keep), the open-source AIOps and
alert management platform. Keep went through Y Combinator (W23) and was
acquired by [Elastic](https://www.elastic.co/). Most of my days are spent
somewhere between backend systems, observability and developer tooling.

This blog is where I write about engineering, open source and whatever else
I happen to be thinking about.

If you want to contact me, you can reach me via one of the following
communication channels:

* find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/shahargl)
* reach out to me on [X](https://x.com/shaharglazner)
* connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shahar-glazner-29294654/)
